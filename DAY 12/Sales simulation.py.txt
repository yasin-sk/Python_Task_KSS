 # Random Sales Simulation
Scenario:
A company wants to simulate 10 days of sales (range 100–500).
Task:
● Generate random integers using NumPy.
● Print the array.
● Find the average sales.


import numpy as np
# 1. Generate 10 random integers between 100 and 500 (inclusive)
sales = np.random.randint(100, 501, size=10)
# 2. Print the generated sales array
print("Simulated 10-day sales:", sales)
# 3. Find the average sales
average_sales = np.mean(sales)
print("Average sales:", average_sales)
