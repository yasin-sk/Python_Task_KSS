 # Temperature Alert System
Temperatures recorded in a city:
temps = np.array([28, 32, 35, 31, 29, 40, 38])
Task:
● Identify days where temperature is greater than 30.
● Return their indices.


import numpy as np
# Given temperatures recorded in a city
temps = np.array([28, 32, 35, 31, 29, 40, 38])
# Identify days where temperature > 30 and return their indices
alert_indices = np.where(temps > 30)[0]
print("Indices of days with temperature > 30°C:", alert_indices)
print("Temperatures on alert days:", temps[alert_indices])
